## Kanto's old water city, underwater

Bubbly Basin is the Expansion Pass's area — a submerged former Cerulean City, reachable only once Ditto has learned the Dive move from a befriended Pokémon.

## What's confirmed here

A distinct subset of Ancient Artifacts — **Sunken Large Lost Relics** (a Windup Inkay, a Chimecho wind chime, a Gyarados fountain, and more) — is exclusive to this area; see the Collectibles page. Other Bubbly Basin-specific collectibles referenced in source material include pearls and treasure maps.
