## A blank canvas

Unlike the other four base areas, Palette Town isn't a ruined former city — it's three vacant islands set aside for free-form play, with no fixed layout to work around. Professor Tangrowth is confirmed to appear in every base area except Palette Town, underlining its different role: a sandbox within the sandbox.

## What's confirmed here

Its Pokémon Center PC Shop unlocks the **Antique table** recipe once the area's own Environment Level reaches 5 — see the Milestones page.
