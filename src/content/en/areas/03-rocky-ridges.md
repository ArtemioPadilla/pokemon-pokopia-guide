## A mountain valley under ash

Rocky Ridges is where Pewter City once stood, now a mountain valley covered in volcanic ash. It's the confirmed source of iron ore and iron ingots — several of the Recipes page's furniture entries (Iron chair, Iron table) list "found at Rocky Ridges" as their material source.

## What's confirmed here

Human Records collectibles confirmed in this area include a newspaper about a new artificial hot spring and a letter titled "You've Been Selected!" — see Collectibles for the sourced list. Its Pokémon Center PC Shop also runs its own Environment-Level-gated unlock track, separate from Withered Wasteland's.
