## A coastline that lost its light

Bleak Beach sits over the ruins of Vermilion City, Kanto's old port town — but where Withered Wasteland's problem is drought, Bleak Beach's is darkness. Sources describe the area as devastated and shrouded, distinct in mood from the cracked-earth wasteland you start in.

## What's confirmed here

Several Human Records collectibles are attributed to this area (see the Collectibles page for the full sourced list, including a newspaper about Vermilion's old rankings and a sailor's diary). The Highlight Reel photo op **"A Head Full of Blooming Flowers"** is tied to a Mosslax encountered after waking in Bleak Beach specifically.
