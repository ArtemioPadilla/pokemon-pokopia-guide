## Saffron City's districts, adrift

Sparkling Skylands reimagines former Saffron City districts as floating islands rather than restoring them in place — the most visually distinct of the base game's areas.

## What's confirmed here

Its Pokémon Center PC Shop unlocks the **Gaming fridge** recipe once the area's own Environment Level reaches 10 — see the Milestones page. That's the highest area-specific level requirement confirmed in this guide's sources, suggesting Sparkling Skylands is meant to be tackled later than Withered Wasteland.
